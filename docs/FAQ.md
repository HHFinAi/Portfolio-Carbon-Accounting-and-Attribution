# Frequently asked questions

## What is the Portfolio Carbon Accounting and Attribution Agent?
An HHFinAi research-workflow package answering: What emissions are financed by the scoped holdings, how complete is the data, and why did the measured inventory change? It contains specialist instructions, executable state controls, bounded calculations and human-review outputs.

## Is this institutional-quality work for the buy side?
It is designed around institutional buy-side process requirements: investment materiality, instrument boundaries, evidence, downside, reproducibility and challenge. It is not independently audited, production-certified or evidence of investment outperformance.

## Does it produce tradable recommendations?
It supports instrument-specific investment-decision handoffs where market/contract evidence and the mandate permit. Research-only and no-investment-route outcomes are valid. Accounting, compliance and stewardship outputs can inform portfolio decisions without being standalone trades. There is no automatic execution.

## Is the workflow auditable?
Local sources, assumptions, calculations, revisions and review decisions are inspectable. Review identity and source truth are not independently authenticated; hashes alone do not provide tamper-proof retention.

## Is an AI model included?
No. An external authorized host AI or a human performs research and submits artifacts. Deterministic demos prove workflow mechanics, not model reasoning, accuracy or agent integration.

## Which standards does it implement?
It references dated primary-source guidance listed in the source register. It does not claim exhaustive standards conformance or legal certification. Executable accounting is limited to long-only listed-company equity and corporate-debt EVIC inputs, not all PCAF asset classes. No short-netting, derivatives, sovereign GDP denominator, nature credits, or avoided-emissions offsets. Methodology changes and scope restatements require manual reconciliation.

## Can it run without paid data?
The tests, calculations and synthetic examples run offline using the Python standard library. Real issuer diligence may require licensed or user-supplied documents and market data. No vendor data are bundled.
