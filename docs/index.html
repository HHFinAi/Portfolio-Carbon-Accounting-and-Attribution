Portfolio Carbon Accounting and Attribution Agent

Institutional-quality buy-side research, designed to support tradable investment decisions through a traceable, auditable workflow.

The decision question

What emissions are financed by the scoped holdings, how complete is the data, and why did the measured inventory change?

Research outputs

  • Separate Scope 1+2 and Scope 3 financed inventories
  • Full-denominator observed contribution and covered-only WACI
  • Coverage and exposure-weighted data quality
  • Reconciled holdings/emissions/EVIC change bridge
Control boundaries

Executable accounting is limited to long-only listed-company equity and corporate-debt EVIC inputs, not all PCAF asset classes. No short-netting, derivatives, sovereign GDP denominator, nature credits, or avoided-emissions offsets. Methodology changes and scope restatements require manual reconciliation. No independent certification, embedded LLM, live feeds or automatic trading.
