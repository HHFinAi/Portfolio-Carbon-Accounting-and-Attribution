## Measurement basis

The portfolio assumes USD 10 million NAV at each year-end. Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, Cisco and IBM weights are respectively **30/25/20/15/10%** in 2023 and **25/20/25/15/15%** in 2024. These are modeling assumptions, not observed account positions. Each issuer's emissions use the same fiscal year as its consolidated revenue. Microsoft and Cisco have non-calendar years; both remain visible in the input table. Later restatements retrieved in 2026 mean the comparison is retrospective, not usable as an historical backtest.

Reported Scope 1 plus market-based Scope 2 has 100% weight coverage. Acquisition reporting timing and operational-control versus consolidated financial boundaries remain material alignment issues for Microsoft and Cisco. Scope 3 is kept separate: IBM's historical cells are unavailable and Meta changes criteria in 2024. The workflow does not impute those gaps, subtract offsets or assign unsupported data-quality scores.

The [source ledger](sources.json) links issuer environmental disclosures, annual reports and SEC filings with table locators. It distinguishes sourced facts from the portfolio assumptions and Meta equity-component inferences. Original document bytes and independent human assurance are not recorded.

## Site evidence, financial mechanism and proposed engagement

**Reported fact:** Meta's environmental index reports **57 megalitres of 2024 water withdrawal at Mesa, Arizona** (printed page I, site table). Withdrawal is not consumption. Earlier dashes are unavailable data. The index's group water-stress figure does not establish Mesa's own hazard classification. This names a real operating location and dependency but does not complete a catchment-level nature assessment.

**Inference to test:** a binding water-supply or cooling constraint could reduce usable compute capacity or defer expansion, affecting operating cash flow. Resilience measures could change capex, opex and contingency capacity. No outage probability, site margin, project benefit or issuer valuation premium is estimated here. A financial scenario requires verified facility/catchment boundaries, permits and utility limits, cooling headroom, intervention costs, useful life and incremental contribution margin. Compare discounted incremental margin and direct cost savings with intervention costs; do not count gross potentially avoided revenue as earnings.
